# Competitive Analysis: Manual Excel/Spreadsheets vs. Material Passport Generator

**Date:** January 25, 2025  
**Competitor Type:** Manual Solutions (Excel/Spreadsheets)  
**Target Market:** Sustainability managers, circular economy consultants, manufacturers

---

## Executive Summary

This analysis compares traditional Excel-based manual solutions for material passport creation against Material Passport Generator, an AI-powered web application. Excel solutions currently dominate the market due to their ubiquity and flexibility, but they face significant limitations in automation, accuracy, and regulatory compliance. The Material Passport Generator offers a modern, AI-driven alternative that addresses these gaps while supporting the EU Digital Product Passport (DPP) mandate for 2026.

**Key Finding:** While Excel solutions are familiar and low-cost initially, they impose massive hidden costs through time consumption (4-8 hours per passport), high error rates (up to 88% of spreadsheets contain errors), and lack of automation—resulting in $50,000+ annual labor costs for organizations creating 100 passports per year.

## 1. Product Offering

### Manual Excel/Spreadsheet Solutions

**What They Provide:**

Excel-based solutions for material passport creation typically offer:

- **Basic Templates**: Pre-formatted spreadsheets with fields for material composition, specifications, and compliance data
- **Manual Data Entry**: Users manually input all material information from technical datasheets, lab reports, and supplier documents
- **Custom Calculations**: Excel formulas for calculating simple metrics (sums, averages, basic ratios)
- **Data Organization**: Structured tables and sheets for organizing multiple materials or products
- **Local Storage**: Files stored on local computers or shared network drives
- **Limited Export**: Basic PDF/print functionality via Excel's export features

**Typical Features:**

- Input validation using dropdown lists and data validation rules
- Conditional formatting for visual indicators (e.g., color-coding)
- VLOOKUP/INDEX-MATCH for referencing material databases
- Pivot tables for summary analysis
- Chart creation for basic visualization
- Macro automation (rarely used due to complexity)

**Example Use Case:** A sustainability manager at a concrete manufacturer downloads an Excel template from industry association, manually inputs cement, water, aggregates, and additives from multiple internal documents, creates simple formulas to calculate total mass, and saves the file as a PDF to submit to regulators.

### Material Passport Generator

**What It Provides:**

- **AI-Powered Input**: Automatic prediction of material properties and sustainability metrics using trained ML models
- **Integrated Calculations**: Automated calculation of compressive strength, recyclability scores, CO2 emissions, and circularity scores
- **Real-time Validation**: Instant validation of material compositions and compliance with regulatory standards
- **Professional Formatting**: Automatically generates professional, standardized digital passports with QR codes
- **Cloud-Based Storage**: Secure cloud storage with shareable URLs
- **Multi-Format Export**: Professional PDF generation, JSON, and CSV formats for different use cases
- **Model Comparison**: Ability to compare predictions from 6 different ML models
- **Sustainability Grading**: Automated A-F grading based on comprehensive sustainability metrics
