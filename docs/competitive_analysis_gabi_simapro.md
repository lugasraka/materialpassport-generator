# Competitive Analysis: GaBi (Sphera) & SimaPro (PRé Sustainability) vs. Material Passport Generator

**Date:** January 25, 2025  
**Competitor Type:** LCA Software (Life Cycle Assessment)  
**Target Market:** Sustainability professionals, LCA experts, enterprises, researchers

---

## Executive Summary

GaBi (Sphera) and SimaPro (PRé Sustainability) are established enterprise-grade LCA software solutions with 30+ years of market presence. Both tools are designed for comprehensive Life Cycle Assessment across product lifecycles, with extensive databases and scientific rigor. However, they are not purpose-built for digital material passport generation and require significant LCA expertise to operate effectively. Material Passport Generator, with its AI-powered approach, targets a different market segment—rapid, accessible passport generation for non-experts in building materials industry.

**Key Finding:** GaBi and SimaPro are powerful LCA solutions for comprehensive environmental impact studies costing €10K-€50K+ annually, requiring weeks of expert training. They lack AI-powered property prediction, rapid passport generation, EU DPP-specific formats, and ease of use for non-LCA experts. Material Passport Generator offers specialized digital passport generation at <1% of the cost (€1K-€20K annually) with AI capabilities and circular economy focus.

## 1. Product Offering

### GaBi (Sphera)

- **LCA For Experts**: Desktop software for precise small-scale assessments (up to 25 LCAs), 45-day free trial

**Features:**
- Full LCA modeling (carbon footprint, global warming potential, land use, acidification, eutrophication, water use)

- Industry-specific models (Chemical, Discrete, Process Manufacturing)
- SpheraCloud platform integration

**Material Analysis Capabilities:**
- Extensive material libraries (concrete, steel, packaging materials)
- Supports Environmental Product Declaration (EPD) generation
- Scenario modeling for design alternatives
- LCA for Packaging specialization

### SimaPro (PRé Sustainability)

**Core Products:**
- **SimaPro Craft** (formerly SimaPro Desktop): Expert LCA software for individual professionals and small teams
- **SimaPro Synergy**: Enterprise solution for scalable LCA integration into daily business processes

**SimaPro Craft Plans:**
- **Expert Plan** (Business): Scenario analysis, library editing, uncertainty calculation, multi-user support
- **Power Plan** (Business): All Expert features + confidential data hiding, Excel/SQL database linking, Report Maker
- **PhD Plan** (Education): Advanced features for comprehensive LCA studies

**Material Analysis Capabilities:**

- Additional free databases: Agribalyse, WEEE, EXIOBASE
- 30+ impact assessment methods (IPCC 2021, ReCiPe 2016, EF 3.1, AWARE, CML-IA, etc.)

### Material Passport Generator

**What It Provides:**

## 10. Conclusion

GaBi (Sphera) and SimaPro are mature, enterprise-grade LCA software solutions optimized for comprehensive life cycle assessment, carbon accounting, and environmental product declarations. Their strengths—scientific rigor, extensive databases, and enterprise scalability—make them ideal for organizations conducting detailed LCA studies and publishing EPDs.

However, **they are not purpose-built for digital material passport generation** and share critical limitations for this use case: high cost (€10K-€50K+ annually), steep learning curves, lack of AI capabilities, absence of EU DPP format support, and focus on comprehensive LCA rather than rapid, accessible outputs.

**Material Passport Generator has a clear competitive advantage** in the emerging market for digital material passports by addressing these gaps: AI-powered property prediction, web-based accessibility, rapid generation, non-expert usability, EU DPP compliance, and circular economy focus. The target audience (sustainability managers, architects, engineers, building material suppliers) is distinct from GaBi/SimaPro's core market (LCA experts, enterprises, researchers).

**Strategic Positioning:** Material Passport Generator should not compete on comprehensive LCA but should lead on rapid, accessible digital material passport generation for circular economy in construction—positioning as a complementary solution that can coexist with GaBi/SimaPro for organizations needing both capabilities.

**Key Takeaway:** The competitive advantage for Material Passport Generator is clear for the digital material passport use case—88-98.8% cost reduction, massive time savings, AI-powered automation, and specialization in the emerging EU DPP market. GaBi and SimaPro will remain the choice for comprehensive LCA studies, while Material Passport Generator can capture the rapidly growing market for accessible, rapid digital passport generation focused on circular economy.
